Frequently asked questions

How does the WordTrove word unscrambler work?

It checks every word in the WordTrove list and keeps the ones you can spell with your letters, using each letter only as many times as you typed it.

Can I use blank tiles?

Yes. Type a question mark (?) for each blank tile or wildcard, up to three.

How many letters can I enter?

Up to 12 letters. Results show words from 3 to 8 letters long, longest first.
