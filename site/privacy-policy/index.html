Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-25. This policy explains what personal data WordTrove ("we", "us") collects when you use https://word-trove.netlify.app, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have wherever you live. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, other US state privacy laws, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, and similar laws in other countries.

Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-up, and nothing you type into the word tools ever leaves your browser.
  • Google Analytics runs only if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Change your mind any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.
  • We do not sell your personal information or use it to profile you. We do not share it for targeted advertising.
  • We honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

Who is responsible

WordTrove is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact us about privacy at hello@wordtrove.app.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 with Google Consent Mode. Until you click Accept, no analytics cookies are set and Google receives only cookieless signals without identifiers, which it uses to estimate overall traffic. After you accept, Google Analytics sets the cookies listed above and records the pages you view, your device and browser type, approximate location and the site that referred you. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We have not enabled Google signals or advertising features, so this data is not used for ads. We keep analytics data for no longer than 14 months.

Google processes this data on our behalf. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Google Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager to manage the measurement tags on this site. Tag Manager itself sets no cookies, but loading it means Google receives your IP address and browser details. Every tag it runs follows your cookie choice: nothing that needs consent fires until you click Accept.

Advertising

We show no ads, set no advertising cookies and do not share data for targeted advertising.

Fonts

Page text is set in webfonts served by Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files directly from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which means Google receives your IP address and browser user-agent. We send no referring URL with those requests and Google Fonts sets no cookies.

Who we share data with

We don't sell, rent or trade personal data. We disclose it only to the service providers below, who process it for the purposes in this policy, or when the law requires it.

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) hosts the site. Netlify privacy policy
  • Google LLC (United States) provides Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google Fonts. Google privacy policy

International transfers

Our service providers are based in the United States, so your data may be processed outside your country. For transfers from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions where the provider is certified, and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses otherwise.

How long we keep data

  • Server logs are kept by Netlify for a limited period under its own retention policy.
  • Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Emails are kept until your question is resolved, and no longer than 2 years.
  • Local storage stays on your device until you clear it, and your cookie choice expires after 12 months.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time with Cookie settings. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that already happened. Email hello@wordtrove.app to make a request. We reply within 30 days, or within 45 days where US state law allows. We may ask a question to confirm the request is yours. Because we collect so little, we often hold no data linked to you, and we will tell you if so. We will never treat you differently for using your rights.

Europe, the UK and Switzerland

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Swiss FADP you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Our legal bases are listed in the table above. You may also complain to your data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK or your national authority listed by the European Data Protection Board.

California and other US states

Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and the privacy laws of states including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon, you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to opt out of its sale, its sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, targeted advertising and profiling. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and we have not done so in the past 12 months.

In the past 12 months we collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (IP address, analytics cookie IDs) and internet activity (server logs, and pages viewed if you accepted analytics), from the sources and for the purposes in the table above. We do not collect sensitive personal information. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) or Do Not Track signal, we treat it as a choice to opt out: Google Analytics stays off and we don't show the cookie banner. You can still turn analytics on with Cookie settings if you want to.

Other countries

People in Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25), Australia (Privacy Act 1988), New Zealand (Privacy Act 2020), India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023), Japan (APPI), South Korea (PIPA), Singapore (PDPA), South Africa (POPIA) and many other countries have similar rights to access, correct and delete personal data, withdraw consent and complain to a regulator. Email us to use them. We handle every request the same way, wherever it comes from.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, uses a strict Content Security Policy and loads scripts only from its own domain and Google. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as possible so there is little to protect.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and change the effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: hello@wordtrove.app.
