Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-24. WordTrove ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use https://word-trove.netlify.app.

Information we collect

We don't offer accounts and don't ask for personal details. Your searches, unscrambles and puzzle guesses are processed entirely in your browser and are never sent to us or anyone else. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP address and requested page, to deliver the site and block abuse. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

Analytics

We run no analytics. We do not use cookies, we set no tracking identifiers, and we do not profile visitors.

Advertising

We show no ads and no advertising cookies are set.

Fonts and other third parties

Page text is set in webfonts served by Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files directly from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which means Google receives your IP address and browser user-agent on each visit. We send no referring URL with those requests and Google Fonts sets no cookies. Google's handling of the data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Local storage

We store your theme choice and your daily puzzle progress, streak and stats in your browser using local storage. This data never leaves your device and we cannot read it. Clearing site data removes it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information. We do not sell or share personal information. To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@wordtrove.app.

Children

The site is for a general audience and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.
