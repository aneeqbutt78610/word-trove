## How to play

Guess the hidden 5 letter word in six tries. After each guess, green tiles are right letters in the right spot, yellow tiles are in the word but in another spot, and grey tiles are not in the word.

Canonical game page: [Daily Word Puzzle](https://word-trove.netlify.app/daily-word-puzzle/)

## Key facts

- Word length: 5 letters. Guesses allowed: 6.
- New puzzle: every midnight in the player's local time zone.
- Guesses are checked against 6,543 five letter words; answers come from a smaller list of 500 common words.
- Streaks and stats are stored only in the player's browser.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does the WordTrove daily puzzle work?

Guess the hidden 5 letter word in six tries. After each guess, green tiles are right letters in the right spot, yellow tiles are in the word but in another spot, and grey tiles are not in the word.

### When is there a new puzzle?

A new word starts at midnight in your own time zone. A countdown under the board shows how long is left.

### Where are my streak and stats saved?

In your browser on this device only. WordTrove has no accounts and does not send your guesses anywhere. Clearing site data resets them.

### Can I play more than once a day?

Yes. After the daily puzzle, press Practice round for unlimited random words. Practice rounds do not change your streak.
