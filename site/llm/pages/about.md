## What is WordTrove?

WordTrove is an independent, free word finder and daily word puzzle for people who play word games. It is not affiliated with The New York Times, Hasbro or Mattel.

## What can I do here?

- Find 3 to 8 letter words with letters you know, using the [5 letter word finder](https://word-trove.netlify.app/) or another length.
- Unscramble letters with the [word unscrambler](https://word-trove.netlify.app/word-unscrambler/).
- Play the [daily word puzzle](https://word-trove.netlify.app/daily-word-puzzle/) and keep a streak.
- Browse [word lists](https://word-trove.netlify.app/word-lists/) by first and last letter.

## Why is it fast?

Every search runs in your browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create, and results appear while you type. Our list covers 54,779 words; [how our word list works](https://word-trove.netlify.app/how-our-word-list-works/) explains where they come from.
