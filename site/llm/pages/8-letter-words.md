## Quick answer

WordTrove's 8 letter word finder searches 17,437 eight letter words. Type letters you know into their positions, add letters that are in the word, and remove letters that aren't. Every matching word appears instantly with its Scrabble score.

Canonical tool: [8 Letter Word Finder](https://wordtrove.app/8-letter-words/)

## How to use the 8 letter word finder

1. Green boxes: type each letter you know into its exact position.
2. In the word: add letters that appear in the word but whose position is unknown.
3. Not in the word: add every letter you have ruled out.
4. Pick a word from the list. Each word shows its Scrabble score.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many 8 letter words are there?

The WordTrove word list has 17,437 playable 8 letter words. 0 of them have no A, E, I, O or U. Counts differ between dictionaries; see how our word list works.

### What is the highest scoring 8 letter word?

QUIZZING scores 36 points with standard English Scrabble tile values, before board bonuses.

### How do I find 8 letter words with these letters?

Type letters you know the position of into the green boxes, letters that are in the word but in the wrong spot into the yellow field, and letters that are not in the word into the grey field. The list updates as you type.

### Is the WordTrove word finder free?

Yes. WordTrove is free, needs no account, and runs searches in your browser on any phone or computer.
